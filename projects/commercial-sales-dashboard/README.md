# Commercial Sales Dashboard

## Context

This was developed as an individual academic project during the Executive MBA in Business Analytics & Big Data at FGV. The original dashboard artifact is in Portuguese.

## Objective

The objective was to explore and communicate commercial sales performance through an analytical dashboard.

## Analytical Scope

The dashboard presents:

- sales performance;
- regional analysis;
- product and item analysis;
- sales representative performance;
- KPI visualization;
- business-oriented data communication.

## Tool

Tableau.
