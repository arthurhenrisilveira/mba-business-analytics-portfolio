# MBA Business Analytics Portfolio

This repository is a curated selection of academic analytics projects developed during an Executive MBA in Business Analytics & Big Data at FGV.

The portfolio focuses on:

- Business Analytics
- Customer and Growth Analytics
- Business Insights
- Strategy & Analytics
- Data Visualization

Projects are selectively included based on authorship, data and privacy considerations, redistribution rights, and relevance to the portfolio. This repository does not represent every project developed during the MBA.

## Projects

- [Commercial Sales Dashboard](projects/commercial-sales-dashboard/README.md) — Academic sales dashboard focused on commercial KPIs, regional performance, product performance and sales representative performance.
